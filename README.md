# Prisoners of Geography — Interactive Diplomatic Atlas

## Purpose
An interactive geopolitical visualization platform inspired by *Prisoners of Geography*, illustrating how mountains, navigable river basins, plains, and narrow maritime chokepoints shape national strategy and global supply chains.

## Key Engineering Highlights
- **Custom SVG World Cartography:** Zero-API-key interactive world map plotting 8 strategic theatres, sea lines of communication (SLOCs), and real-time disruption alert rings.
- **Chokepoint Disruption Simulator:** Models global oil flow impact, transit delay/savings days, rerouting protocols, and combined trade exposure across major maritime bottlenecks.
- **Automated QA:** Unit-tested region filtering and scenario impact calculations via **Vitest** (see `TEST_REPORT.md`).
